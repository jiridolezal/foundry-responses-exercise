# Voltava Energie – Electricity Price List 2026 (households)

*Voltava Energie a.s. is a fictional company. All data in this document is made up for training purposes.*

Valid from 1 January 2026 until further notice. Prices include VAT (21 %).

## Products

| Product | Type | Energy price (CZK/MWh) | Fixed monthly fee (CZK) | Contract length | Notice period |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Volt Fix 24** | Fixed price | 3 890 | 149 | 24 months | 3 months before end of contract |
| **Volt Fix 12** | Fixed price | 4 120 | 149 | 12 months | 3 months before end of contract |
| **Volt Flex** | Variable, follows spot market monthly average + 450 CZK/MWh margin | spot + 450 | 99 | Indefinite | 1 month |
| **Volt Green** | Fixed price, 100 % renewable (certificates of origin) | 4 350 | 179 | 24 months | 3 months before end of contract |

Distribution charges are set by the Energy Regulatory Office and billed separately (not included above).
For the calculations in this document, use an average distribution charge of **2 250 CZK/MWh** (distribution rate D02d).

## How your monthly advance is calculated

Monthly advance = (annual consumption in MWh × (energy price + distribution charge)) / 12 + fixed monthly fee

Example: 3.0 MWh per year on Volt Fix 24:
(3.0 × (3 890 + 2 250)) / 12 + 149 = 1 535 + 149 = **1 684 CZK per month**

## Early termination

- Fixed-price products (Volt Fix 12, Volt Fix 24, Volt Green): early termination fee of **500 CZK per remaining month**, maximum 6 000 CZK.
- Volt Flex: no termination fee.
- Moving house: no fee if the customer moves to a place where Voltava Energie cannot supply, with proof of the move.

## Discounts

- **E-invoice discount:** 30 CZK per month if the customer receives invoices electronically only.
- **Loyalty discount:** 3 % off the energy price after 5 years as a customer (not combinable with Volt Flex).
