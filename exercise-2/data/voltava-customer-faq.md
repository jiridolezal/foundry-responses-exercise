# Voltava Energie – Customer FAQ

*Voltava Energie a.s. is a fictional company. All data in this document is made up for training purposes.*

## Billing

**How often do I get a bill?**
Once a year, after the annual meter reading. During the year you pay monthly advances. If you paid more than you
used, the overpayment is returned within 15 days of the annual bill; if less, the underpayment is due within 14 days.

**Can I change my monthly advance?**
Yes, once per quarter, in the customer portal (Moje Voltava) or by phone. The new advance must cover at least 80 % of
the expected consumption.

**Why is my bill higher than last year?**
The most common reasons: higher consumption (check the meter readings on the bill), a change of product (e.g. from a
fixed price to Volt Flex during a period of high spot prices), a change in regulated distribution charges, or the end
of a discount.

## Contracts

**How do I cancel my contract?**
In writing through the customer portal, by e-mail to smlouvy@voltava-energie.example, or by post. Observe the notice
period of your product (see the price list). Fixed-price contracts renew automatically for 12 months unless cancelled
in time.

**Can I switch products?**
Yes. Switching from Volt Flex to a fixed-price product is possible any time, effective from the 1st of the next month.
Switching between fixed-price products is only possible at the end of the contract period.

## Complaints

**How do I file a complaint?**
Through the customer portal, by e-mail to reklamace@voltava-energie.example, or at a customer centre. Include your
customer number, the invoice number and a description of the issue.

**How long does it take?**
We confirm receipt within 2 working days and resolve the complaint within **30 days**. Billing complaints must be filed
within **60 days** of the invoice date. If you disagree with the result, you can turn to the Energy Regulatory Office.

## Outages

**What do I do if the power is off?**
Planned outages are announced at least 15 days in advance on the website and by SMS. For an unplanned outage, contact
the distribution system operator's emergency line (non-stop). Voltava Energie is a supplier and doesn't operate the
grid, so we can't fix outages, but we can tell you the current status.
